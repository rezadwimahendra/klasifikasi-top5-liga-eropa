# Tugas 1: Dataset Public, Topik, dan Judul Penelitian

## 1. Dataset Public yang Digunakan
- **Nama Dataset:** Top 5 European Football Leagues Match Statistics (Musim 2025/2026)
- **File Dataset:** `data/data_top_5_liga.csv`
- **Sumber:** Open Public Sports Data Repository (Kaggle / Football-Data.co.uk)
- **Ukuran & Sampel:** 1.752 baris pertandingan (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1)
- **Atribut Utama (22 Kolom):**
  - **Identitas Match:** `Date`, `HomeTeam`, `AwayTeam`
  - **Skor & Hasil Target:** `FTHG` (Full Time Home Goals), `FTAG` (Full Time Away Goals), `FTR` (Full Time Result: `H`=Home Win, `D`=Draw, `A`=Away Win)
  - **Statistik Babak Pertama:** `HTHG` (Half Time Home Goals), `HTAG` (Half Time Away Goals), `HTR` (Half Time Result)
  - **Statistik Pertandingan:**
    - `HS`, `AS`: Total Tembakan (Home / Away Shots)
    - `HST`, `AST`: Tembakan Tepat Sasaran (Shots on Target)
    - `HF`, `AF`: Pelanggaran (Fouls)
    - `HC`, `AC`: Tendangan Sudut (Corners)
    - `HY`, `AY`: Kartu Kuning (Yellow Cards)
    - `HR`, `AR`: Kartu Merah (Red Cards)
